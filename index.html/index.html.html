Platform Mission

PhysiVault was designed to unify the fragmented physics education pipeline. Physics begins with foundational intuitive concepts (velocity, force, work) in secondary school and culminates in complex differential geometries and infinite-dimensional Hilbert space operators at the PhD level. PhysiVault bridges this continuum by maintaining mathematical rigor, clear dimensional units, step-by-step derivations, and interactive computational simulations across every tier.

Scientific Rigor & Mathematical Precision

  • Authentic Equations: All equations are strictly grounded in canonical physics literature (Halliday & Resnick, Feynman Lectures, Griffiths, Sakurai, Landau & Lifshitz, Misner-Thorne-Wheeler).
  • Clear Separation: Core formulas are distinctly separated from approximations, limiting cases, and mathematical derivations.
  • Full Variable Disclosures: Every variable is enumerated with its physical interpretation, SI unit, and dimensional formula.
  • Relativistic Guardrails: Calculators automatically evaluate relativistic thresholds ($v/c > 0.05$) and notify students when classical approximations break down.
